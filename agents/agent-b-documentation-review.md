TRANSCRIPT AND BLOCKERS
Read any supplied transcript named in request.target_context, and independently compare its relevant segments with upstream observations. Only mark a human question blocking=true for the exact decision it prevents. Missing downstream execution evidence need not stop article inventory, visible-control comparison, or readability review. Complete supported work and leave other claims unverified.
PURPOSE-AWARE ARTICLE QUALITY
Identify the document's actual purpose in the existing quality.reason field before rating it. Assess a release note as a release note, a how-to as a how-to, and a reference as a reference. Lack of a full procedure does not by itself make a release note poor quality: use NOT_APPLICABLE for procedural criteria outside its purpose, and explain separately in why_relevant whether it can answer this user task. Likewise, a broad user-creation article does not need a standalone section for every setting unless a concrete reader need justifies it.
Unknown roles, validation, seasonal exceptions and enforcement do not automatically establish defects. Rate UNVERIFIED where their relevance or correct behavior is unknown; NOT_APPLICABLE where outside purpose. A confirmed omission can justify improvement only with a concrete reader consequence. Never invent an exception or prerequisite in order to fail a criterion.
MISSING LINKED GUIDANCE
FOUND remains appropriate when at least one relevant article was read, even if another linked guide is unavailable. Search completeness means completed target search of the supplied corpus, not all linked external content. Before routing work, assess whether an unread linked guide could materially change coverage or the canonical update destination. If so use HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED for the final route, cite the known evidence and request that guide or an attributed owner decision. Keep any proposed update conditional; do not assert a company-wide gap or automatically create a duplicate article. This does not prevent reporting supported local findings.

Do not put phrases such as "the supplied video does not demonstrate saving" into a customer-facing scope_outline. The outline describes confirmed product guidance or explicitly conditional topics to confirm, not pilot investigation notes. No article is written or published.
